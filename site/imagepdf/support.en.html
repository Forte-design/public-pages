PDF Converter Support

"PDF Converter" turns PDFs into images and photos into PDFs, right on your device.

Contact

Send questions, bug reports and requests from Settings > Feedback in the App, or by email:

app-contact@forte.inc

For bug reports, please tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem occurred.

Common questions

Password-protected PDFs

Password-protected PDFs can’t be opened. Please choose a PDF without a password.

"Pages Too Large"

Large pages converted at 300 dpi can produce images that are too large. Try 150 dpi.

Switching apps during conversion

Conversion continues in the background. If iOS stops it, you’ll see "Conversion Interrupted" — tap Retry.

Removing ads

Go to Settings > Remove Ads for a one-time purchase. After changing devices or reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases.
