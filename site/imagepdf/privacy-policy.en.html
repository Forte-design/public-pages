Privacy Policy (PDF Converter)

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy describes how Forte, Inc. ("we") handles information in the iOS app "PDF Converter" (the "App").

Photos and PDFs

The App converts PDFs to images and photos to PDFs. All conversion happens on your device. We never send the photos or PDFs you choose, the converted files, or their file names to our servers or anywhere else.

  • You choose photos with the iOS photo picker. The App receives only the photos you select and does not request access to your whole photo library.
  • You choose PDFs with the iOS file picker. The original files are not modified.
  • Results are saved where you choose in the iOS save screen. The App does not request permission to add to your photo library.
  • When creating a PDF from photos, location and other metadata in the photos are not included in the PDF.
  • Temporary files created during conversion are kept on the device and deleted when you close the result or the next time the App starts.

Information stored on your device

  • Default conversion settings (image format, resolution, PDF paper size)
  • Records used to limit how often ads appear (number of completed conversions and when an ad was last shown)
  • Your ad consent status and purchase status
  • The type of the most recent conversion or save failure (attached to feedback diagnostics only; no file names)

This information is not sent off your device.

Sending feedback

Only when you send feedback from the App, the following is sent to us. Nothing is sent unless you send feedback.

  • The text you enter and the type you choose
  • Only if "Attach Diagnostics" is on (off by default): app version, iOS version, device model, language, and the type of the most recent failure

Photos, PDFs and file names are never sent. Feedback goes through a relay server we operate (Cloudflare Workers) to our internal messaging tool (Slack). We use it only to review your feedback and investigate problems, never for advertising or tracking, and we do not share it with third parties. We generally keep it for up to one year.

Advertising

The App is free. While free, it may show an ad while a conversion is in progress. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect device information, approximate location, app usage and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads. The App does not ask for tracking permission.
  • In regions such as the European Economic Area, a consent screen is shown through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy.
  • See Google's policy for how AdMob handles information.

Purchases

We offer a one-time in-app purchase that removes ads. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store; we never receive payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device using information from the App Store and is not sent elsewhere.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law. Photos and PDFs are never sent to us or anyone else. Feedback passes through the services of providers we use to send and store it (Cloudflare, Inc. / Slack Technologies).

Children

The App is not specifically designed for children.

Deleting data

Deleting the App removes all information it stores on your device. Delete saved results from the app where you saved them (such as Files). To request deletion of feedback you sent, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed and will announce important changes on this page.
