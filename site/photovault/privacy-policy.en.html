Privacy Policy

FORTE, Inc. | September 8, 2026

Photos, videos and keys
Selected media is encrypted on your device. We do not request access to read your entire photo library or delete the originals. We do not store or transmit your passcode. Keys are protected using the iOS Keychain.
If you enable iCloud backup, encrypted media and metadata are stored in your private iCloud database. Recovery keys are synced through iCloud Keychain. Photos and videos are not sent to FORTE servers. Apple’s terms apply to its services. Turning backup off does not delete backups already stored in iCloud.

Permanently deleted photos are also removed from iCloud when the next backup completes successfully. Deletions cannot reach iCloud while backup is off or the connection fails. Deleted photos are not retained in the app and deletion cannot be undone.

Feedback
Feedback you choose to submit is delivered to FORTE through a Cloudflare relay and Slack. If you enable diagnostics, the app and iOS versions, device model and language are included. We use this information to investigate problems and improve the app. Photos, filenames, passcodes and keys are not attached. Do not include personal or confidential information in your message. Individual replies may not be available.

Ads and purchases
The free version uses Google AdMob. Google may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses and ad interactions for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Tracking follows your iOS permission. You can decline and continue to use all free features. Photos, videos and passcodes are not provided to the advertising SDK. Consent is requested where required and privacy options are available in Settings where applicable.
Purchases are processed by Apple. FORTE does not receive payment card details.
Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your choices
You can share or delete media. Removing this app deletes local media; check that backups and recovery keys are available first. Apps and recipients you share with operate under their own terms.

Contact and changes
Use Feedback & Bug Reports in Settings to contact us. Updated versions of this policy will be published in the app.
