Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy
FORTE, Inc. | September 27, 2026

This policy applies to Private Album for iPhone and iPad (iOS) and for Android. Points that differ between the two are listed separately under “iOS” and “Android”.

Photos, videos and keys
Selected media is encrypted on your device. We do not request access to read your entire photo library or delete the originals. We do not store or transmit your passcode. Photos and videos are not sent to FORTE servers.

iOS
Keys are protected using the iOS Keychain.

Android
Media is received through the Android photo picker or shared from other apps. The app has no permission to read your photos, videos or storage, and does not write to your device’s photo library. The key used for biometric unlock is protected by the Android Keystore.
Encrypted media and key data are excluded from Android device backup and from data transfer to a new device. After changing devices or reinstalling, restore from the Google Drive backup described below.
App screens are blocked from screenshots, screen recordings and the recent apps overview. As a result, photos in the app cannot be captured in screenshots either.

Intruder photos
Camera access is requested only when you turn on “Take an intruder photo”. After two incorrect passcode attempts in a row, the front camera takes a photo, which is encrypted and saved on this device. These photos are not sent to FORTE and are not included in the app’s backups.

Backup
iOS (iCloud)
If you enable iCloud backup, encrypted media and metadata are stored in your private iCloud database. Recovery keys are synced through iCloud Keychain. Photos and videos are not sent to FORTE servers. Apple’s terms apply to its services. Turning backup off does not delete backups already stored in iCloud.

Android (Google Drive)
If you enable Google Drive backup, data is stored in a private area of Google Drive reserved for this app, in the Google Account you choose on Google’s consent screen. The only access the app requests is to read and write this app-specific area (drive.appdata). It does not read other files in your Drive.
Stored data consists of encrypted media and album information, backup management information (item counts, sizes, dates and device model) and recovery keys. So that you can recover your photos if you forget your passcode or change devices, recovery keys are stored in the same Google Account. Photos and videos are not sent to FORTE servers. Google’s terms apply to its services. Turning backup off does not delete backups already stored in Google Drive. You can delete backups by date, or all of them, from “Backup status” in the app.

Both
Permanently deleted photos are removed from the latest backup when the next backup completes successfully. They remain in earlier dated backups (up to 10 generations) until that date is no longer kept or you delete that backup. Deletions cannot reach the cloud while backup is off or the connection fails. Deleted photos are not retained in the app and deletion cannot be undone.

Feedback
Feedback you choose to submit is delivered to FORTE through a Cloudflare relay and Slack. If you turn on “Include device diagnostics”, the app and OS versions, device model, language and numbers describing the open album’s storage (item counts, sizes, album count, sort order and the results of storage and backup checks) are included. On iOS this is the iOS version and device model; on Android it is the Android version (API level) and the manufacturer and model. We use this information to investigate problems and improve the app. Photos, filenames, album names, passcodes and keys are not attached. Do not include personal or confidential information in your message. Individual replies may not be available.

Ads
The free version uses Google AdMob. Google may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses and ad interactions for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Photos, videos and passcodes are not provided to the advertising SDK. Consent is requested where required and privacy options are available in Settings where applicable.

iOS
Tracking follows your iOS permission. You can decline and continue to use all free features.

Android
The advertising ID, approximate location inferred from your IP address, ad interactions such as views and taps, and crash and diagnostic data from the advertising SDK may be sent to Google. You can change advertising ID settings in your device settings. After you purchase Premium, the advertising SDK is not started.

Purchases
iOS
Purchases are processed by Apple. FORTE does not receive payment card details.

Android
Purchases are processed by Google Play. FORTE does not receive payment card or other payment details, and purchase records are not sent to FORTE servers.

Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your choices
You can share or delete media. Removing this app deletes local media; check that backups and recovery keys are available first. Apps and recipients you share with operate under their own terms.

Contact and changes
Use Feedback & Bug Reports in Settings to contact us. Updated versions of this policy will be published in the app.
